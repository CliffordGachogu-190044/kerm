Who we are

Kerm Designer ("we", "us") operates two stores in Nairobi: the Town Branch at Hazina Towers, opposite Anniversary Towers, and the Kilimani Branch at Argwings Arcade Mall, Shop No. 89. You can reach us by phone or WhatsApp on +254 720 841678, or on Instagram at @reginakerm254designers. This policy explains how we handle information when you visit this website or contact us through it.

Information we collect

This website has one form: the enquiry form on the homepage. That form does not submit anything to us or store anything on this website. When you fill it in and tap send, it opens WhatsApp on your own device with your message pre-filled, and you choose whether to actually send it from there.

Because of that, the only personal information we receive from this site is whatever you choose to send us directly, such as:

  • Your name, phone number, or Instagram handle, when you message us on WhatsApp or Instagram.
  • Details you choose to share about an order, such as the item you're asking about or a delivery address.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts of any kind. See our Cookie Policy for the full explanation.

Third-party services and embeds

This site's fonts are hosted on this site itself, not loaded from Google or any other third party, so visiting this page does not send your information to a font provider.

Links to WhatsApp, Instagram, and Google Maps on this site are outbound links, not embeds. Clicking one takes you directly to that service in a new tab. We don't receive anything from that click, and once you're on their site, their own privacy policy applies rather than this one.

How we use your information

We use the information you share with us to respond to enquiries, arrange and fulfil orders, and provide customer support. We do not use it for anything beyond running our business and communicating with you, and we do not sell it.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with delivery or logistics partners solely to fulfil an order, and only to the extent necessary.

Children's privacy

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. To make a request, contact us using the details below.
